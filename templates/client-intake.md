# Client intake — UK partner route

> Agent: ask only what's missing. Max 8 questions per message, numbered, answerable in a few words.

## Quick 8 (ask first)
1. Where is the applicant now — outside the UK or inside? If inside, on what visa, expiring when?
2. Sponsor's status: British / ILR / settled status / refugee?
3. Married, civil partners, engaged, or unmarried? Since when? How long together?
4. Sponsor's income: employed / self-employed / director / pension / other — gross per year, how long in this job?
5. Any savings (amount, held how long, whose name)?
6. Where will you live? (own / rent / family) How many bedrooms & people?
7. English: test passed (which level)? Or English-speaking nationality / English-taught degree?
8. Any previous visa refusals, overstays, criminal records, or previous marriages? (yes/no — details later)

## Follow-ups (as relevant)
- Date first partner visa granted (transitional £18,600 check)
- Children (ages, nationality, where they live)
- Sponsor on Carer's Allowance / PIP / DLA / Attendance Allowance?
- Sponsor currently living abroad? UK job offer?
- Time physically together; visits; cohabitation history
- TB-listed country residence (≥ 6 months)?
- Health conditions relevant to Art 8 / English exemption
- Planned application date

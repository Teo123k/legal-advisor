# Authoritative sources & how to verify

Order of authority (highest first). When sources conflict, the higher one wins.

| # | Source | What it is | URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary legislation | Immigration Act 1971; Immigration and Asylum Act 1999; Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Part 5A = s.117A–D); Borders, Citizenship and Immigration Act 2009 (s.55 children); Immigration Act 2014 | legislation.gov.uk |
| 2 | Human Rights Act 1998 / ECHR Art. 8 | Right to respect for private and family life | legislation.gov.uk |
| 3 | **Immigration Rules** | Appendix FM (family), Appendix FM-SE (specified evidence), Part Suitability, Appendix English Language, Appendix Finance, Appendix Private Life | gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules |
| 4 | Statements of Changes (HC papers) | Every rule change + commencement & transitional provisions | gov.uk/government/collections/immigration-rules-statement-of-changes |

| 7 | GOV.UK public pages | Fees, IHS, processing times, how to apply | gov.uk/uk-family-visa · gov.uk/government/publications/visa-regulations-revised-table |

## Verification protocol (for the agent)

1. Find the rule paragraph on the **current** Immigration Rules page and quote it verbatim in working notes.
2. For case law: open the judgment, confirm neutral citation, court, date, and the paragraph you rely on.
   Then set `status: verified (YYYY-MM-DD)` in `07-case-law.md`.
3. For figures: confirm on GOV.UK and update `volatile-facts.md`.
4. If a practitioner blog says something the rules don't, treat it as **unverified**.
5. Record what you checked in `cases/<name>/working/sources.md` (URL + date + quote).

# Financial Requirement — the #1 refusal reason

> Rules: Appendix FM E-ECP.3.1–3.3 / E-LTRP.3.1–3.3; evidence: **Appendix FM-SE**;
> Home Office guidance *Appendix FM 1.7: financial requirement*. Figures: `volatile-facts.md`.

## Thresholds
- **£29,000** gross annual income — applications under the post-11 April 2024 rules. No extra amount for children.
- **£18,600** (+£3,800 first child, +£2,400 each further non-British/non-settled child) — **transitional**:
  applicant already holds partner leave granted under pre-11 April 2024 rules, same partner.
- Savings alone: **£16,000 + 2.5 × (threshold − income)**. Savings-only at £29,000 = **£88,500**.
  At ILR, multiplier is **1** (£16,000 + shortfall).

## Whose income counts
| Source | Counts? |
|---|---|
| Sponsor's employment / self-employment / pension / non-employment income | ✅ |
| Applicant's income — **only** if in the UK with permission to work at the date of application | ✅ (in-country) / ❌ (from abroad, except some overseas-sponsor-returning cases) |
| Joint savings of applicant and/or sponsor | ✅ |
| Third-party gifts, family support, promises of support | ❌ under rules — only via **GEN.3.1** (exceptional) |
| Prospective/future job offer for the **applicant** | ❌ under rules — GEN.3.1 only |
| Most state benefits | ❌ (except listed ones that **exempt** from MIR and some e.g. maternity/bereavement pay that count) |
| Loans, credit | ❌ |

## Categories (FM-SE)

| Cat | Who | Test | Core evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Salaried employee with **same employer ≥ 6 months** | Current gross annual salary ≥ threshold (and paid at that rate for the 6 months) | 6 months payslips; 6 months bank statements showing matching net pay; employer letter (FM-SE para 2(b)): confirms employment, gross annual salary, length of employment, period paid at that salary, type of contract |
| **B** | Employed **< 6 months** with current employer, or variable/salary changed | (i) current salary ≥ threshold **and** (ii) total gross employment income in prior 12 months ≥ threshold | 12 months payslips + bank statements + employer letter(s) |
| **C** | Non-employment income: rental, dividends (not from own Ltd co. – that's F), interest, maintenance, trust | Total in last 12 months ≥ threshold (may combine with A/B/D/E) | Property ownership proof, tenancy agreements, statements showing receipt, tax evidence |
| **D** | **Cash savings** held ≥ 6 months, in name of applicant/sponsor/joint, under their control | £16,000 + 2.5 × shortfall | 6 months statements (all pages), source of funds if recently deposited/transferred; overseas funds converted at OANDA rate on application date |
| **E** | Pension (state, occupational, private) | Annual amount ≥ threshold (combinable) | Pension statement/award letter + bank credits |
| **F** | Self-employed / director of specified limited company — **last full financial year** | Gross income ≥ threshold in last full FY | SA302/tax calculation + tax year overview, accounts, business bank statements, company registration, payslips/dividend vouchers, etc. |
| **G** | Same as F — **average of last 2 full financial years** | Average ≥ threshold | As F for 2 years |

**Combining:** A/B income can be combined with C, D, E. F/G have specific combination rules — **check FM-SE
paras 7–9 and 18–20 before combining self-employed income with anything else.**

## Sponsor returning to the UK with the applicant (entry clearance)
Sponsor working abroad can rely on: (a) **overseas employment** meeting the threshold for the relevant period
(6 / 12 months), **and** (b) a **confirmed UK job offer** starting within 3 months of return at ≥ threshold.
Evidence: overseas payslips/statements + UK offer letter/contract with salary & start date.

## Evidence traps (check every time)
1. Payslips without matching **bank credits** (cash-in-hand) → ⚠️/❌.
2. Statements/letters dated outside the **28 days before application** window (FM-SE guidance: most recent payslip/statement should be dated no earlier than 28 days before application — verify current wording).
3. Bank statements not on letterhead / online printouts not stamped — FM-SE accepts e-statements **with** supporting letter/stamp or as per guidance.
4. Employer letter missing **one** of the required items → common refusal.
5. Salary changes during period → may push from Cat A to Cat B.
6. Self-employed: figures don't match HMRC → serious risk (suitability + *Balajigari* fairness).
7. Savings recently transferred in → must show the **6-month history** of the source account.
8. Gross figures used (not net). Overtime/bonus counts only if evidenced and (Cat A) consistently.
9. Maternity/sick leave periods: special rules — use pre-leave salary in some cases (check guidance).

## Exemption: disability/carer benefit
If sponsor receives a listed benefit (see `01-requirements.md` R4.2), MIR does not apply. Instead show
**adequate maintenance**: net income after housing costs ≥ what a family would get on Income Support/Universal Credit
equivalent (*Ahmed (benefits: proof of receipt; evidence) [2013] UKUT 84* — `verify`). Evidence: award letters, statements.

## When the money isn't there — GEN.3.1
If refusal would breach Article 8 (unjustifiably harsh consequences, e.g. British child's best interests),
the decision-maker **must** consider: credible third-party support, applicant's credible prospective
earnings, other credible reliable sources (*MM (Lebanon) [2017] UKSC 10*). Leads to the 10-year route.
Evidence of third-party support must show it's credible and sustainable (sponsor's own finances, legal
undertaking, history of support).

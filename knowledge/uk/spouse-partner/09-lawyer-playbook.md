# Lawyer Playbook — how immigration lawyers build a spouse visa file

## Step 1 — Intake & conflict check (day 1)
- Full history: both partners' immigration history, refusals, convictions, previous marriages, children,
  travel, employment, savings. Use `templates/client-intake.md`.
- Identify the **route** and **stage**; check transitional rules by date of first partner grant.
- Spot red flags early (AGENTS.md §4).

## Step 2 — Requirement mapping
- Build the requirement grid (R1–R9 in `01-requirements.md`). Decide the **financial category** first —
  it dictates the timing (e.g. wait until 6 months with the employer for Cat A).

## Step 3 — Timing strategy
- Choose the application date so that **all** evidence windows line up (6/12 months payslips, 6 months
  savings, documents within 28 days, English test valid, 2-year unmarried period complete).
- Watch rule-change dates (fee rises in April; settlement English B2 from 26 Mar 2027; earned settlement).

## Step 4 — Evidence collection & QC
- Request exact documents with a checklist; reject non-compliant ones (e.g. employer letter missing gross salary).
- **Reconcile money line by line:** each payslip net → bank credit, same date ± days, same employer name.
- Translate & certify; check names/dates match passports.

## Step 5 — Write legal representations (cover letter)
Standard structure (template: `templates/cover-letter-spouse.md`):
1. Introduction: who, what route, stage, which paragraphs relied on.
2. Summary table: requirement → met? → evidence ref.
3. Requirement-by-requirement submissions — short, factual, cite the rule.
4. **Address weaknesses head-on** (gap in employment, name discrepancy, previous refusal and what changed).
5. Exceptional circumstances / Art 8 (only if needed) — cite case law, attach expert evidence.
6. Conclusion & request.
7. Evidence index.

**Lawyer style:** neutral, precise, no adjectives, no begging, no emotional essays. Facts → rule → evidence.

## Step 6 — Client statements
- Witness statements in the client's own words (lawyer edits for clarity, never invents).
- Cover: how met, relationship development, wedding, time together/apart and why, life plans in UK.
- Must be consistent with the form and all documents.

## Step 7 — Pre-submission audit
- Second-person review against checklist; recalculate finances; check every date.
- Form answers vs evidence vs statements: **zero contradictions**.

## Step 8 — After decision
- Grant: diarise extension date (apply in last 28 days before expiry… check current guidance) and ILR planning;
  English level for next stage; keep evidence of cohabitation for next stage.
- Refusal: `06-refusals-and-fixes.md`; deadline in calendar on day of receipt.

## Typical lawyer judgement calls
| Situation | Typical approach |
|---|---|
| Salary just under threshold | Top up with Cat D savings (calc shortfall × 2.5 + £16k) rather than argue |
| New job < 6 months | Cat B if 12-month total works; otherwise wait |
| Self-employed first year | Usually wait for full financial year + tax return filed |
| Short relationship, quick marriage | Extra relationship evidence + statements explaining timeline |
| Applicant visitor in UK wanting to switch | Advise return & apply from abroad unless genuine EX.1 facts |
| Previous refusal | Quote reasons and show precisely how each is now addressed |
| Overstayer with British partner | Honest EX.1/GEN.3.2 assessment; usually 10-year route; manage expectations |

# Say / Don't Say — content rules for cover letters & statements

> Golden rule: **Disclose everything the form and rules require. Remove everything that doesn't help
> prove a requirement or explain a weakness.** "Don't say" never means "hide".

## ✅ ALWAYS include
| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Which route, stage and paragraphs you rely on | Caseworker knows exactly what to assess |
| Every requirement mapped to evidence reference | Speeds decision, prevents "not provided" refusals |
| Exact financial category + calculation (gross figures) | Prevents miscategorisation |
| Explanation of every discrepancy (names, dates, gaps, address changes) | Pre-empts suspicion |
| Previous refusals + what's changed | Mandatory disclosure; shows good faith |
| Immigration history accurately (incl. overstays, breaches) | Mandatory; non-disclosure = deception risk |
| Criminal convictions/cautions if asked | Mandatory |
| Previous marriages and how they ended | Required for R3.7 |
| Children (all, including from other relationships) | Required; best-interests analysis |
| Intention to live together permanently in the UK, with concrete plans | R3.10 |
| How applicant will be supported **without public funds** | Core requirement |

## ✂️ REMOVE / don't write
| Item | Why | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Plans or hopes to claim benefits / social housing | Contradicts "no recourse to public funds"; may cause refusal | Show income & accommodation |
| Third-party money as if it counts under the rules ("my parents will support us") | Doesn't count under rules; looks like you don't meet them | Only via GEN.3.1 with structured evidence if rules can't be met |
| Emotional pleas, love letters, religious/poetic text | Not evidence; dilutes the file | 1-page factual statement each |
| Speculation ("I might start a business", "maybe we'll move to Dubai") | Undermines intention to live in UK / financial stability | Concrete, current facts |
| Criticism of the Home Office, politics, threats to go to the press/MP | Unhelpful tone | Neutral legal submissions |
| Irrelevant private details (health of unrelated relatives, intimate details) | Privacy; noise | Only health facts relevant to Art 8/exemption, with medical evidence |
| Inconsistent dates or "approximately" for key events | Credibility | Exact dates checked against documents |
| Unverifiable claims ("we talk 10 hours a day") | Credibility | Evidence samples |
| Admissions you haven't checked (e.g. "I worked a bit while on a visit visa") | Could be a breach — needs legal assessment first | 🔴 Stop — refer to regulated adviser **before** drafting; the truth must still be disclosed where required, but how it's framed needs a lawyer |
| Other people's personal data beyond what's needed | GDPR/privacy | Redact irrelevant third-party data |
| Mass dumps (300 chat pages, 200 photos) | Caseworker can't find key evidence | Curated samples with index |
| Statements that you "don't know" the partner's family/job details | Relationship doubt | Make sure statements show real knowledge (true facts) |

## ✍️ Rewrite patterns (Before → After)

**Money**
> ❌ "My husband earns good money and his family is rich so we will be fine."
> ✅ "The sponsor has been employed by [Employer] since 01/02/2025 on a gross salary of £32,500 p.a.
> (Category A, FM-SE para 2). Evidence: payslips [C1–C6], bank statements [C7], employer letter [C8]."

**Relationship**
> ❌ "We are deeply in love and cannot live without each other."
> ✅ "We met on 14/03/2023 in [City] (photos [B3]). We married on 20/07/2024 ([B1]). Since then we have
> spent 5 months together across 3 visits ([B5] boarding passes) and speak daily ([B6] call log samples)."

**Gap explanation**
> ❌ (silence about a 4-month gap in bank credits)
> ✅ "Between 01/2025 and 04/2025 the sponsor was on statutory maternity leave; FM-SE para 2 allows reliance
> on salary before leave. Evidence: employer letter confirming leave and pre-leave salary [C9]."

**Previous refusal**
> ❌ "We applied before and they wrongly refused."
> ✅ "A previous application was refused on 05/02/2026 under E-ECP.3.1 because the employer letter omitted the
> gross salary. This application includes a compliant employer letter [C8]. All other requirements were accepted."

**Accommodation**
> ❌ "We will live with my parents, there is enough space."
> ✅ "The couple will occupy a bedroom exclusively at [address], a 4-bedroom house owned by the sponsor's
> father (title [D1]), occupied by 4 adults. The owner consents [D2]. The property is not overcrowded (inspection report [D3])."

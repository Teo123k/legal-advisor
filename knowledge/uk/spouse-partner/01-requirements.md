# Requirements — clause by clause (requirement grid source)

> Use this file to build the ✅/⚠️/❌ grid in Mode A. Paragraph numbers are Appendix FM
> (entry clearance `E-ECP`, leave to remain `E-LTRP`, ILR `E-ILRP`). **Paragraph numbering can be
> re-cut by Statements of Changes — confirm against the current rules before quoting to the Home Office.**

## R1. Validity (form, fee, IHS, biometrics, passport)
- Correct form for the stage; fee + IHS paid; biometrics enrolled; valid passport/ID.
- Invalid applications are rejected without a decision (no appeal). Check before anything else.

## R2. Suitability (Part Suitability — applications on/after 11 Nov 2025)
- Mandatory/discretionary refusal for: custodial sentences (≥ 12 months → mandatory), persistent offending,
  deception/false representations (incl. in previous applications), breach of immigration laws
  (overstaying beyond permitted grace, illegal working, absconding), litigation/NHS debts ≥ threshold,
  exclusion/deportation orders, conduct not conducive to the public good.
- Human-rights override: where refusal would breach ECHR, route-specific provisions allow grant.
- **Agent rule:** any suitability issue = 🔴 red flag → regulated adviser.

## R3. Relationship (E-ECP.2.1–2.10 / E-LTRP.1.2–1.12)
| # | Requirement | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| R3.1 | Sponsor is British / settled / refugee / HP (or qualifying EU/forces status) | Passport, BRP/eVisa share code, naturalisation cert |
| R3.2 | Applicant and partner both **18+** at date of application | Passports |
| R3.3 | Not within **prohibited degrees** of relationship | Declaration |
| R3.4 | Have **met in person** | Photos, travel records, wedding evidence |
| R3.5 | Relationship **genuine and subsisting** | See `04-relationship-evidence.md` |
| R3.6 | If married/civil partners: **valid marriage/CP** recognised in UK | Certificate (+ certified translation), registration |
| R3.7 | Any **previous relationship** of either has **broken down permanently** | Final order / decree absolute / death certificate |
| R3.8 | Unmarried partners: relationship akin to marriage for **≥ 2 years** before application | Evidence across the full 2 years; explain periods apart |
| R3.9 | Fiancé(e): genuine intention to marry within 6 months of arrival | Wedding plans, venue booking, notice-of-marriage plan |
| R3.10 | **Intend to live together permanently in the UK** | Statements, accommodation, sponsor ties to UK |

## R4. Financial (E-ECP.3.1–3.4) → full detail `02-financial-requirement.md`
| # | Requirement |
|---|---|
| R4.1 | Gross annual income ≥ **£29,000** (or transitional £18,600 + children) via Categories A–G **or** savings ≥ £16,000 + 2.5 × shortfall |
| R4.2 | **OR** exemption: sponsor receives a listed benefit (Carer's Allowance, DLA, PIP, Attendance Allowance, Armed Forces Independence Payment / Guaranteed Income Payment, Industrial Injuries Disablement Benefit, Constant Attendance Allowance, War Pensions Mobility Supplement) → then **adequate maintenance** test |
| R4.3 | Evidence strictly as per **Appendix FM-SE** |

## R5. Accommodation (E-ECP.3.4 / E-LTRP.3.4)
- Adequate accommodation for the family **without recourse to public funds**, owned or occupied
  **exclusively** by the family (shared kitchen/bathroom with others is OK if a room is exclusive).
- Not **overcrowded** (Housing Act 1985 Part X — room & space standards) and no public-health contravention.
- Evidence: title deeds/mortgage statement or tenancy agreement; landlord/owner consent letter if living
  with family; optional property inspection report (recommended where several people live there).

## R6. English language (E-ECP.4.1–4.2 / E-LTRP.4.1–4.2 / Appendix English Language)
- Entry: **A1** speaking & listening (approved SELT, from approved provider); Extension: **A2**; ILR: **B1** (+ Life in the UK test). **B2** for settlement from 26 Mar 2027.
- Met automatically: national of a majority-English-speaking country; degree taught in English
  (Ecctis confirmation if non-UK); previously met at the required level on this route.
- Exemptions: aged 65+; disability (physical/mental condition preventing it — medical evidence);
  exceptional circumstances preventing test before arrival.

## R7. Immigration status (in-UK applications only — E-LTRP.2.1–2.2)
- Applicant must **not** be in the UK as a **visitor**, with leave of **≤ 6 months** (other than fiancé(e)),
  on **immigration bail**, or **in breach of immigration laws** (ignoring short permitted overstay periods)
  — **unless EX.1 applies** (`05-article8-exceptions.md`).

## R8. Children (if included)
- Each child must meet Appendix FM child requirements (age, not living independently, adequate
  maintenance/accommodation, sole responsibility rules if one parent). For transitional cases,
  child amounts add to £18,600. Best interests of the child: s.55 BCIA 2009, GEN.3.3.

## R9. Other
- TB test certificate if resident ≥ 6 months in a listed country (entry clearance).
- Certified translations of every non-English/Welsh document (translator's confirmation, date, name, contact).
- Knowledge of Language & Life in the UK (KoLL) at ILR.

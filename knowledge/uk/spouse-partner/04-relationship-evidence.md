# Genuine & Subsisting Relationship

> Rule: Appendix FM E-ECP.2.6 / E-LTRP.1.7 (and 2-year akin-to-marriage test for unmarried partners, GEN.1.2).
> Guidance: *Family Policy — Genuine and subsisting relationship* (Home Office). Case law: `07-case-law.md`.

## What the caseworker is asking
1. Is this a **real** relationship (not a sham/arranged for immigration)?
2. Is it **subsisting** — ongoing *now*, not just a valid legal marriage on paper? (*GA (Ghana)*, *Goudey*)
3. Do they **intend to live together permanently in the UK**?
Standard of proof: **balance of probabilities**; burden on the applicant. If the Home Office alleges a
**sham marriage / marriage of convenience**, the legal burden of proving that is on the Home Office
(*Agho*, *Rosa* — EEA context but routinely applied by analogy; `verify` for Appendix FM).

## Factors in Home Office guidance (positive indicators)
- Have lived together / do live together; length of relationship
- Shared finances, joint responsibilities, shared tenancy/bills
- Children together
- Time spent together & visits; **frequent communication** across time
- Knowledge of each other's personal circumstances, family, history
- Family/friends know about and support the relationship (wedding attendance, photos)
- Plausible, consistent account of how they met and developed the relationship
- Cultural context explained (e.g. arranged marriage is **not** a negative factor in itself)

## Risk indicators caseworkers look for (address them head-on)
- Very short courtship / marriage soon after meeting, especially if applicant's leave is expiring
- Large age gap with no context; no common language
- Inconsistent accounts between partners (dates, places, how met)
- Applicant has history of previous sponsored marriages, or sponsor sponsored previous partners
- Little or no time physically together; no wedding photos with family
- Payment for marriage; dowry concerns explained poorly
- Marriage referred under the **sham marriage referral scheme** (Part 4, Immigration Act 2014)

## How lawyers present relationship evidence (quality > quantity)
1. **Short chronology table** in the cover letter: date · event · evidence ref.
2. **Samples, not dumps:** communication evidence = representative screenshots from each period
   (first contact, regular contact, around visits, recent), with dates visible. Not 300 pages.
3. **Photos:** 10–20, captioned (who, where, when), spread across the relationship, including family.
4. **Explain the gaps:** periods apart due to work/visa/COVID/study — give reason + proof of contact.
5. **Consistency check:** both statements, the form, and the evidence must tell the same story.
6. **Intention to live in UK:** sponsor's job/home/family in UK; applicant's plans (job search, study).

## Unmarried partners — since 31 Jan 2024
Cohabitation for 2 years is **no longer mandatory**, but you must still show a relationship **akin to
marriage for ≥ 2 years** — commitment, shared life, financial interdependence, future plans. If not
living together, explain why (work, culture, religion, visa restrictions) and evidence continuity.

## Interview prep (if invited) — honest preparation only
Remind both partners to review *their own* true chronology (dates of meeting, wedding, visits). Never script
answers. Differences in minor details are normal; contradictions on major facts are fatal.

# UK Spouse / Partner Route — Overview

> Rules: Immigration Rules **Appendix FM** (Section EC-P entry clearance, R-LTRP leave to remain,
> R-ILRP settlement) + **Appendix FM-SE** (evidence) + **Part Suitability** (from 11 Nov 2025).
> Figures → `../../_meta/volatile-facts.md`.

## Who can use it
The applicant's **partner (sponsor)** must be: a British citizen; settled in the UK (ILR / EUSS settled
status); in the UK with refugee status or humanitarian protection; or certain Appendix EU / armed forces
categories. "Partner" = spouse, civil partner, fiancé(e)/proposed civil partner, or **unmarried partner**
(relationship akin to marriage for ≥ 2 years — cohabitation no longer mandatory since 31 Jan 2024).

## The journey (5-year route)

```
Entry clearance (33 months)  ──►  Extension FLR(M) (30 months)  ──►  ILR after 60 months
      A1 English                      A2 English                       B1 English (B2 from 26-Mar-2027)
      £29,000 / savings               financial req. again             financial req. again + KoLL test
```
Fiancé(e) visa: 6 months to marry, then switch in-country to spouse leave (30 months).

**10-year route:** granted where rules aren't fully met but refusal would breach Article 8
(EX.1 insurmountable obstacles / GEN.3.2 unjustifiably harsh). ILR after 120 months, 30-month grants.

## Eligibility triage — decision tree (Mode E)

```
1. Is sponsor British / settled / refugee / HP?                      no → ❌ wrong route
2. Both 18+? Met in person? Relationship genuine & subsisting?        no → ❌ / ⚠️ evidence
3. Married/civil partners (valid in law) OR unmarried ≥ 2 yrs OR fiancé(e)?  no → ❌
4. Previous relationships permanently ended (final order/decree)?     no → ❌
5. Money: £29,000 (or transitional £18,600) via Categories A–G,
   OR sponsor on a listed disability/carer benefit (adequate maintenance)?  no → go 5a
   5a. Third-party support / applicant's prospective earnings / other credible source
       → only via GEN.3.1 if refusal = unjustifiably harsh            → 🔴 exceptional route
6. Adequate accommodation, not overcrowded, no public funds?          no → ❌
7. English: A1 test / exempt nationality / English-taught degree / exemption?  no → ❌ (book test)
8. Suitability: criminality, deception, immigration breaches, debts to NHS?  issue → 🔴 red flag
9. In UK? Current immigration status allowed (not visitor / ≤6m / bail / overstayer)?
   no → EX.1 (insurmountable obstacles / child) or GEN.3.2               → 🔴 10-year route
```

## Best route selector

| Situation | Usual route |
|---|---|
| Applicant abroad, all requirements met | **Entry clearance — spouse/partner** (5-yr) |
| Not yet married, want to marry in UK | **Fiancé(e) visa** → switch after marriage |
| Applicant in UK on a qualifying visa (e.g. Skilled Worker, Student — check), rules met | **Switch in-country (FLR(M))** |
| Applicant in UK as visitor / ≤ 6 months leave | Usually must leave & apply from abroad (EX.1 exception rare) |
| Applicant overstayer, British partner, insurmountable obstacles | **FLR(M) under EX.1** → 10-yr route |
| Financial req. not met, strong Art. 8 case (children, disability) | **GEN.3.1 / GEN.3.2** → 10-yr route |
| Sponsor is EEA national with pre-settled/settled status | Check **Appendix EU (Family Permit)** eligibility first — different rules |

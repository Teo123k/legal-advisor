# 06 — Overstayer in UK with British partner (EX.1)

**Facts:** Applicant (Nigerian) entered as visitor 2019, overstayed. Met British partner 2021, married 2023.
Partner has multiple sclerosis, receives PIP, treated at a specialist UK clinic; partner's teenage son
from a previous relationship lives with them part-time under a child arrangements order.

**Issues:** Immigration status requirement fails (overstayer) → EX.1(b); s.117B(4) little weight to
relationship formed while unlawful; insurmountable obstacles; suitability (overstay) — 🔴 red flag.

**Analysis**
- Financial: partner on PIP → MIR exemption, adequate maintenance test.
- EX.1(b) **two-stage test (Lal)**: partner's MS + specialist treatment continuity + medication availability
  in Nigeria + child arrangements order (partner can't relocate the child / leave him) → potentially
  very significant difficulties that couldn't be overcome or would entail very serious hardship.
- Evidence needed: consultant's letter (prognosis, treatment, consequences of disruption), country
  evidence on MS treatment availability/cost, the court order, son's views/school.
- Public interest: overstay counts heavily; *Agyarko* — precarious/unlawful → exceptional circumstances.

**Strategy:** Regulated adviser essential. FLR(M) relying on EX.1 and GEN.3.2; expect 10-year route.

**Verdict:** 🔴 HIGH RISK (arguable with strong medical + child evidence).

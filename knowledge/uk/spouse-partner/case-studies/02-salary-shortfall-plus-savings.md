# 02 — Salary below threshold + savings

**Facts:** Sponsor earns £25,000 gross (Cat A, 2 years same employer). Joint savings £27,500 held 8 months.

**Calculation (entry clearance, multiplier 2.5):**
- Shortfall = £29,000 − £25,000 = £4,000
- Savings needed = £16,000 + (2.5 × £4,000) = **£26,000**
- Held £27,500 for ≥ 6 months → ✅

**Issues:** Savings must be in name of applicant/sponsor/joint, held 6 months, cash, under their control;
statements must cover full 6 months; any large deposit needs source trail.

**Common mistakes:** using the savings figure on the date of application only (must be ≥ required amount
throughout 6 months — check balance never dropped below); including ISA/stocks not readily accessible
(cash only — check guidance on investments); savings from a gift deposited 2 months ago (fails 6-month rule).

**Strategy:** Show the calculation in the cover letter. Provide all 6 months of statements, all pages.

**Verdict:** 🟡 → 🟢 once the 6-month balance history is confirmed.

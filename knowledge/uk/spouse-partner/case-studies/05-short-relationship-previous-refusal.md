# 05 — Quick marriage, refused on "genuine and subsisting"

**Facts:** Met online Jan 2025, met in person once (2 weeks) Jun 2025, married Jul 2025 in Pakistan.
Applied Sep 2025; refused: caseworker "not satisfied relationship genuine and subsisting" citing few photos,
no evidence of contact, and inconsistent dates on the form.

**Issues:** Weak relationship evidence; inconsistency; appeal vs fresh application.

**Analysis**
- Rules otherwise met (finance ✅, English ✅, accommodation ✅).
- Genuineness is a factual question where **oral evidence at appeal** is powerful; inconsistencies can be
  explained in witness statements. If finance is ✅, appeal succeeds if relationship accepted (*TZ (Pakistan)*).
- Fresh application option: add chronology, communication samples from Jan 2025 → now, proof of further
  visits, wedding photos with families, both statements; explain the form error.

**Strategy:** If another visit is possible → fresh application strengthened (often faster). If not, appeal
(28 days out-of-country) with bundle + witness statements. Arranged/quick marriages are **not** inherently
suspicious — explain cultural context.

**Verdict:** 🟡; mention appeal deadline prominently.

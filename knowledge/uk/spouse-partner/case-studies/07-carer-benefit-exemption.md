# 07 — Sponsor receives Carer's Allowance (MIR exemption)

**Facts:** British sponsor cares full-time for disabled mother; receives Carer's Allowance and part-time
earnings £9,000. Lives with mother in her council flat (2 bedrooms). Applicant in India.

**Issues:** Exemption from £29,000; **adequate maintenance** test; accommodation in social housing
(no *additional* recourse to public funds; space/overcrowding).

**Analysis**
- Carer's Allowance is a listed benefit → MIR doesn't apply; must show adequate maintenance: household net
  income after housing costs ≥ the equivalent income-related benefit level for the family.
- Accommodation: mother's tenancy; landlord (council) permission may be needed for an additional occupant;
  2 bedrooms for 3 adults — check overcrowding standard (couple can share a room).
- Applicant would not be entitled to public funds; household must not claim more because of them.

**Strategy:** Award letters, bank statements, calculation table in cover letter; council consent letter;
inspection report.

**Verdict:** 🟡 — succeeds with a careful maintenance calculation.

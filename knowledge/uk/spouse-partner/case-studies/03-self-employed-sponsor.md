# 03 — Self-employed sponsor

**Facts:** Sponsor is a self-employed electrician (sole trader) for 3 years. Last tax year net profit
£31,000; previous year £24,000. Recently amended previous year's return upward.

**Issues:** Category F (last full financial year) vs G (2-year average = £27,500 → fails). Amended return →
discrepancy risk.

**Analysis**
- Use **Cat F**: last full FY £31,000 ≥ £29,000 → ✅ if evidence complete.
- Evidence: SA302 / tax calculation + tax year overview, business accounts, business & personal bank
  statements for the FY, proof of ongoing self-employment (invoices, contracts, insurance), HMRC registration.
- Amendment: explain with **accountant's letter** (why, when, tax paid). Unexplained amendments close to
  an application attract suspicion (*Balajigari* — fairness, but don't rely on it; explain up-front).

**Strategy:** Apply after the latest return is filed; reconcile accounts ↔ SA302 ↔ bank deposits.

**Verdict:** 🟡 — documentation-heavy; high refusal rate when anything is missing.

# 04 — British sponsor living abroad, returning together

**Facts:** British sponsor has lived in Dubai 6 years, earns AED equivalent of £45,000. Married to a
Jordanian national. Plan: move to UK together. Sponsor has a UK job offer at £31,000 starting in 2 months.

**Issues:** Financial rules for returning sponsors; intention to live in UK; accommodation before arrival.

**Analysis**
- Sponsor can rely on overseas salaried employment at threshold (6 months, Cat A-equivalent) **and**
  a confirmed UK job offer ≥ threshold starting within 3 months of return. Both must be evidenced.
- Accommodation: arranged before arrival (tenancy/family consent letter).
- Intention: job offer + accommodation + return plan demonstrates.

**Strategy:** Offer letter must state salary, start date, role; overseas payslips + statements + employer
letter; convert currency properly; explain timeline.

**Verdict:** 🟡 — usually fine if the job offer is genuine and dates align.

# 01 — Salaried sponsor, applicant abroad (the "clean" case)

**Facts:** British sponsor, NHS nurse, same employer 3 years, gross £34,200. Married 18 months; applicant in
Philippines; 3 visits totalling 7 months; wedding in Manila with families. Applicant has IELTS Life Skills A1.
Renting a 2-bed flat alone.

**Issues:** FM-SE compliance (Cat A); relationship evidence; accommodation; TB test.

**Analysis**
- Financial: Cat A (≥ 6 months with employer, salary > £29,000). Need 6 months payslips + matching bank
  credits + employer letter with all items. ✅ if complete.
- Relationship: married, met in person, visits evidenced, wedding photos with family → strong.
- Accommodation: tenancy in sponsor's name, 2 bedrooms for 2 adults → ✅.
- English ✅. TB test (Philippines is a listed country) → must include.

**Lawyer strategy:** Short cover letter with requirement table; indexed bundle; check payslip ↔ bank credit
dates; ensure last payslip within 28 days.

**Outcome drivers:** Refusals in clean cases are almost always **missing FM-SE items** (e.g. employer letter
missing "length of employment"). Verdict 🟢 once evidence QC passes.

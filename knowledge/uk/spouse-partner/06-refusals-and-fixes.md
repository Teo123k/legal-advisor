# Common Refusal Reasons → Fixes

| # | Refusal reason (as worded in decisions) | Root cause | Fix (fresh application) | Challenge? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You have not provided the specified evidence under Appendix FM-SE" | Missing employer letter item, missing bank statements, wrong period | Re-submit with complete FM-SE set; checklist each item | Appeal possible (human rights) but fresh app usually faster |
| 2 | "Income does not meet £29,000" | Wrong category, net used instead of gross, salary change, cash wages | Recalculate under correct category; combine with savings (Cat D); wait until 6/12 months history | Appeal only if strong Art 8/GEN.3.1 |
| 3 | "Not satisfied relationship is genuine and subsisting" | Thin evidence, inconsistencies, short courtship | Chronology + targeted evidence + both statements; explain red flags | **Appeal often good** — oral evidence at hearing is persuasive |
| 4 | "Not satisfied previous relationship has permanently broken down" | No final divorce order | Obtain final order/decree absolute; then re-apply | — |
| 5 | "Accommodation not shown to be adequate" | No landlord consent; overcrowding | Consent letter + inspection report | — |
| 6 | "English requirement not met" | Wrong test/provider, expired, level too low | Book approved SELT at right level | — |
| 7 | "Immigration status requirement not met (E-LTRP.2.x); EX.1 does not apply" | In UK as visitor/overstayer | Apply from abroad, or build real EX.1/GEN.3.2 case | Appeal if strong insurmountable-obstacles/children case |
| 8 | Suitability — deception / false documents | Fake bank docs, undisclosed refusals/convictions | **Do not re-apply without regulated adviser** — ban periods may apply | Appeal/JR — specialist |
| 9 | Suitability — criminality / immigration breach | Convictions; overstays > permitted period | Specialist advice | Specialist |
| 10 | "Not satisfied you intend to live together permanently in UK" | Sponsor living abroad, no UK plans | UK job offer/home/return plan evidence | Appeal possible |
| 11 | Unmarried: "not in relationship akin to marriage for 2 years" | Short or thin history | Wait until 2 years with evidence; or marry | — |

## Challenge routes
| Route | When | Deadline | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Appeal to First-tier Tribunal (IAC)** | Spouse/partner refusals are **human rights claims** → right of appeal (NIAA 2002 s.82) | **14 days** (in UK) / **28 days** (outside UK) from decision | Ground: refusal breaches Art 8 (HRA 1998 s.6). Rules met = weighty (*TZ (Pakistan)*). Oral evidence possible. Takes months. |
| **Administrative review** | Only where decision is not a human-rights decision (rare for partner route) | 14 days (in UK) / 28 days (outside) | Caseworking error only |
| **Judicial review** | No appeal/AR available, or unlawful process | Promptly, within 3 months | Upper Tribunal; specialist |
| **Fresh application** | Fixable evidence gaps | Anytime (fees again) | Often fastest for evidence refusals |

## Decision rule for the agent (Mode D)
- Evidence gap only, rules can now be met → **recommend fresh application** (state what to add), and
  mention appeal as alternative if timing/costs favour it.
- Caseworker clearly wrong on evidence that *was* submitted, or relationship doubted → **appeal is strong**.
- Rules can't be met but strong Art 8 facts (British child, serious illness, danger abroad) → **appeal**, 🔴 lawyer.
- Any suitability/deception refusal → 🔴 lawyer immediately; do **not** re-apply blindly.
